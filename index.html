Ritmo: Habit Tracker

Offline-first habit tracking & Material You streaks

Privacy Policy · Last updated: November 24, 2026

Your habits stay on your phone. Ritmo has no account, no ads, and no backend server of its own. All of your habits, streaks, check-ins, quit trackers, notes, custom themes, and settings are stored exclusively on your device. The only data that leaves your phone is what Google Play and RevenueCat use to manage optional Ritmo Pro purchases and anonymous entitlement checks (section 3).

1. What stays on your phone

Your habits, schedules, completion history, skip/rest days, reflection notes, Material You color preferences, and reminder times are saved only in the app's private local storage on your Android device. The developer has no access to them. Optional JSON backups and CSV exports are generated locally on your device and shared only where you choose. Uninstalling Ritmo or clearing app data in Android settings permanently deletes all local data.

2. Permissions and what each one is for

  • Notifications & Exact Alarms (optional): delivers local Android habit reminders at the times you choose. Reminders are scheduled entirely on your device.
  • Vibration: provides subtle Material Design 3 haptic feedback on check-ins and controls.
  • Google Play Billing: enables optional Ritmo Pro monthly or lifetime purchases, restores, and Play Store promo code redemptions.

3. Third-party services

  • Google Play and RevenueCat handle optional Ritmo Pro purchases (monthly subscription or one-time lifetime unlock), subscription restoration, and anonymous active-install entitlement checks. The RevenueCat SDK generates a random anonymous App User ID and processes purchase history and basic app/device metadata under the Google Privacy Policy and the RevenueCat Privacy Policy. Payment details are processed solely by Google Play; the developer never sees your card or billing details.

Ritmo contains zero ads, does not use the Android Advertising ID (AAID), and does not track you across other apps or websites.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with advertisers or data brokers. Data is transmitted only to Google Play and RevenueCat strictly as service providers for billing, fraud prevention, and entitlement management as described in section 3.

5. Deleting your data

To delete all habits, history, notes, and preferences stored on your device, use Settings > Start fresh (clear all) inside Ritmo, clear app storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app.

To request deletion of purchase and anonymous app user records stored by RevenueCat, email rabpaulodev@gmail.com with the subject "Ritmo data deletion" and your Google Play order number (GPA.…). Records are deleted within 30 days. Google maintains payment records under the Google Privacy Policy. Deleting records does not cancel an active subscription; to cancel or manage a subscription, open Google Play > Payments & subscriptions.

6. Children

Ritmo is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy is updated, the revised version will be published on this page with an updated date.

8. Contact
